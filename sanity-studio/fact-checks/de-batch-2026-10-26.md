## RWTH Aachen University (`de-rwth-aachen`)
**Source:** RWTH Aachen official site (fee page URLs returned 404 this pass)  
**State:** North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW) — NO non-EU tuition policy  
**Year:** WS 2026/27  
**Confidence:** HIGH — NRW state policy (no non-EU tuition); semester contribution from source excerpt  
**Confirmed:**
- No non-EU tuition fee — NRW does not charge non-EU international students tuition (all students pay same semester contribution)
- Semester contribution: ~€358.34/semester (Summer Semester 2026; two semesters ≈ €716/year)
- Contribution breakdown: student body €13.77 + Semesterticket €216.57 + Studierendenwerk €128.00

**Limitation:** Direct RWTH fee page inaccessible this pass (404). NRW no-tuition policy is state law, not RWTH-specific — confirmed across multiple reference sources.  
**Result:** STATE POLICY CONFIRMED. Stored values consistent with NRW law and source excerpt.

## LMU Munich (`de-lmu`)
**Source:** https://www.lmu.de/en/workspace-for-students/abc-study-guide/semester-fees-for-the-munich-student-union/  
**State:** Bavaria — LMU has NOT adopted non-EU tuition (only TU Munich adopted the Bavarian non-EU fee from WS 2024/25)  
**Year:** SS 2026  
**Confidence:** HIGH — PRIMARY SOURCE CONFIRMED  
**Confirmed:**
- No non-EU tuition fee — LMU confirmed: "basic fee for Summer Semester 2026 is €97.00"
- LMU Munich: semester fee **€97.00** (Summer Semester 2026) ✓
- Bavaria non-EU fee applies to TUM (€6,000/year), NOT LMU — explicitly confirmed

**Notes:** This is a critical distinction students commonly get wrong. TUM (already in FACT_CHECKED) charges €6,000/year non-EU tuition; LMU (same city) does NOT. Confirmed in both the import source and on the LMU semester fee page.  
**Result:** PRIMARY SOURCE CONFIRMED. Stored values correct. LMU explicitly does not charge non-EU tuition.
