## Year-Mismatch Note (Applies to All 4 Schools)
All schools have `decision_context_year` = "Semester 1 February 2028 entry". Students entering Feb 2028 pay **2028** academic year fees. As of 2026-10-26, **no Australian university has published 2028 international fee schedules**. Stored values use 2027 rates (the most current published data). Expected 2028 rates: approximately 3–5% higher based on historical indexation patterns.

This is analogous to the UK/Netherlands year-mismatch situation, but unlike those cases, we **cannot apply a correction** because 2028 fees are not yet published. The stored 2027 rates are the best available data and should be displayed with a note that fees are subject to annual increase.
