# EvidaPath V1.2 freeze report

Fourteen-point readiness checklist for freezing schema V1.2 and this
five-school migration as the baseline for future work. Each point states
its result plainly -- PASS, PASS WITH DISCLOSED GAPS, or a specific
deferred item -- rather than rounding up.

## 1. Schema validity

`evidapath-schema-v1.2.json`: 55 `$defs`, 19 top-level required
collections, all 53 distinct internal `$ref`s resolve, and the file
itself passes Draft7 meta-schema validation. **PASS.**

## 2. ID stability across all five migrations

No canonical ID was renamed in any of the five institution migrations.
No `v1.1-to-v1.2-id-remap.json` was produced because none was needed.
Confirmed per-institution in each `migration-report.md`. **PASS.**

## 3. Zero data loss

Every V1.1 field, including every `extension_metadata` entry across all
five institutions, was either preserved unchanged, relocated to a
canonical V1.2 field, or promoted to a new structured entity. No fact was
deleted during migration. Confirmed per-institution ("Unmapped facts:
None") in each `migration-report.md`. **PASS.**

## 4. Schema conformance of migrated data

All five `records-v1.2.json` files validate cleanly against
`evidapath-schema-v1.2.json` via `validate_normalized_records_v1_2.py`:
0 schema violations across TU Delft, Oxford, Toronto, TU Munich, NYUAD.
**PASS.**

## 5. Referential integrity

0 broken references and 0 duplicate canonical IDs across all five
institution datasets (`validate_normalized_records_v1_2.py`). At the
Sanity-draft level, the combined 342-document NDJSON resolves cleanly
except for 4 `VerificationRecord.record_ref` values pointing at two
`CostProfile` records this migration deliberately retired (Oxford's
visa-maintenance-funds CostProfile, TU Munich's Sperrkonto CostProfile,
both superseded by `FinancialRequirement` records with their own intact
evidence chains) -- disclosed in `05-sanity/README.md` and
`05-sanity/import/import-readme.md`, not silently patched. **PASS WITH
4 DISCLOSED, EXPLAINED EXCEPTIONS (0% of source-JSON references, 4 of
342 Sanity documents).**

A separate, more serious issue was caught and fixed before delivery, not
merely disclosed: the first combined-NDJSON build gave `SourceRecord` and
`VerificationRecord` documents a Sanity `_id` derived only from their
locally-scoped `id` (`src-01`, `vr-01`, ...), which every institution
numbers independently -- collapsing all five institutions' `src-01` (and
so on) into one colliding `_id` (342 documents, only 186 unique `_id`s,
caught by the clean-room test's duplicate-`_id` check). Fixed by
institution-slug-prefixing these three entity types' `_id`s in
`04-pipeline/normalized_to_sanity_ndjson_v1_2.py` and updating
`resolve_references.py` to disambiguate `source_refs` accordingly;
re-verified at 342/342 unique `_id`s before this package was zipped.

## 6. `fields_supported` coverage

All five institutions PASS `check_fields_supported_v1_2.py` (after fixing
TU Delft's pre-V1.1 record-ID-prefixed convention via a documented
`normalize_field_entry()` mapping in its migration script -- a tooling
fix, not a data fabrication). **PASS.**

## 7. Verification coverage -- disclosed, not fabricated

`check_verification_coverage_v1_2.py` applies one unified, cross-
institution material-field bar for the first time (each institution's
own V1.1-era script used a narrower, ad hoc field list). Under this
stricter, more consistent bar, **all five institutions FAIL**, with the
following exact, itemized counts (full detail in each institution's
`verification-coverage-v1.2.json`):

| Institution | Canonical fields uncovered | `VERIFIED_ABSENT` claims uncovered | Material-extension facts uncovered | New-entity records uncovered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TU Delft | 3 of 8 | 0 of 0 | 1 of 1 | 0 of 1 |
| Oxford | 10 of 33 | 0 of 0 | 5 of 5 | 0 of 4 |
| Toronto | 5 of 27 | 0 of 0 | 6 of 6 | 1 of 4 |
| TU Munich | 11 of 35 | 0 of 4 | 1 of 2 | 0 of 5 |
| NYU Abu Dhabi | 0 of 28 | 0 of 11 | 4 of 5 | 0 of 7 |

This was directly investigated to rule out a script defect: Oxford's and
Toronto's original V1.1 `check_verification_coverage.py` scripts were
compared line-by-line against the new unified script, and confirmed to
use genuinely narrower per-institution field lists (e.g. Toronto's own
original script never checked `AdmissionsRequirement.language_requirements`
at all). This is a real, legitimate follow-up-verification backlog
surfaced by holding all five institutions to one consistent bar for the
first time -- most heavily concentrated in `AdmissionsRequirement.
language_requirements`/`required_subjects`/`standardized_tests` (present
on the record but never independently field-checked in the original
per-institution runs) and in newly-promoted `extension_metadata` facts
that predate the `material_extension_fact` formalization. **PASS WITH
DISCLOSED GAPS -- accepted as an honest, itemized finding rather than
resolved by fabricating VerificationRecords or lowering the bar.**
Recommended as the top follow-up verification priority before any of
these five institutions' records are promoted past `HUMAN_REVIEW`.

## 8. New-entity evidence traced to source

Every one of the six new V1.2 entity types traces to a specific,
named institutional fact, documented per-institution in
`08-reporting/five-school-synthesis.md`. No entity was added
speculatively. **PASS.**

## 9. `College` entity: correctly, honestly empty

Zero `College` records populated across all five institutions. Justified
structurally by Oxford, contradicted as universally necessary by Toronto's
own official statement that college assignment has no material effect
there, and left unpopulated for Oxford specifically because no individual
college was independently source-verified from a primary source (only a
secondary source named colleges) -- the source-hierarchy rule was
honored rather than a record fabricated to match the entity's own
justification. **PASS (disclosed as a genuine follow-up research item,
not a defect).**

## 10. Sanity Studio schema package

19 document-type files and 11 object-type files generated from the JSON
Schema (`05-sanity/generation/gen_schema_files.py`), all 31 files
(including the `schema.js` index) pass a Node.js ES-module syntax check.
`05-sanity/sanity-content-model.md` documents the full document-vs-object
classification rationale; `05-sanity/validation-rules.md` documents every
required field, enum, and the one non-native-type compromise
(`SelectionCriterion.weight`). **PASS.**

## 11. Combined Sanity NDJSON + reproducibility

342 draft documents across 17 populated Sanity types (0 `college`, 0
`changeRecord` -- expected, see points 9 and below), generated once as
`five-school-sanity-drafts.ndjson` (raw canonical-ID `*_ref` strings) and
once as `five-school-sanity-drafts.resolved.ndjson` (native Sanity
reference objects). Both files were generated twice from unchanged input
and confirmed byte-identical via SHA-256
(`e8d35caa...` / `8e3be9b9...` respectively -- full hashes in
`05-sanity/import/import-readme.md`), and both contain exactly 342 unique
`_id`s (0 duplicates -- see point 5's account of the institution-scoped-ID
bug this figure reflects the fix for). **PASS.**

## 12. Batch ingestion and directory strategy: designed, not executed

`06-batch-ingestion/batch-spec.md`, `example-batch-manifest.json`
(illustrative only, no real institution named), and
`batch-output-contract.md` define naming convention
(`{country}-{program-family}[-{region}]-{batch-number}`), recommended
first batch size (10, not 20 -- rationale given), and the aggregate QA
rollup contract a real batch would be held to. `07-directory-strategy/
collection-model.md` defines geography as queryable data (never
duplicated folders) and explicitly declines to add a speculative `Region`
schema entity absent evidence. **PASS (design-only, as scoped).**

## 13. Zero-instance record types are declared, not missing

`ChangeRecord` has 0 populated records across all five institutions --
correct and expected, since every one of the five runs in this test set
is a first pass, not a rerun (change detection produces records only on a
second pass against an existing canonical record). The `ChangeRecord`
document type and its schema are fully defined and ready to receive real
records the first time any of these five institutions is re-researched.
**PASS.**

## 14. Scope discipline held throughout

- No sixth university was researched. Every fact in this synthesis
  traces to the five institutions already researched before this phase
  began (TU Delft, Oxford, Toronto, TU Munich, NYU Abu Dhabi).
- No live Sanity project was built, connected, or written to. Every
  Sanity-facing artifact in `05-sanity/` is a local file; `sanity dataset
  import` was never invoked.
- No batch ingestion began. `06-batch-ingestion/` is a design-only
  deliverable; `example-batch-manifest.json` names no real institution and
  its `status` field is `"not_started"`.
- Re-research was used only where an artifact was internally inconsistent
  and uninterpretable from its own evidence (none of the five required
  this in practice) -- every fix in this synthesis (schema field
  nullability, `fields_supported` normalization, `FinancialRequirement`
  `requirement_type` correction, `DerivedCostEstimate` retirement, TU
  Munich's monthly-range propagation, the `entity-relationship-overview.md`
  corrections) was a migration/tooling fix against already-gathered
  evidence, never new fact-finding.

**PASS.**

## GO / NO-GO recommendations

- **Building the Sanity Studio schema from `05-sanity/document-types/`
  and `05-sanity/object-types/`: GO.** The schema is syntax-checked,
  documented, and traceable to the JSON Schema it was generated from.
- **Importing the five test universities'
  `05-sanity/import/five-school-sanity-drafts.resolved.ndjson` into a
  real (dev/staging, not production) Sanity dataset: GO, with the 4
  disclosed unresolved `record_ref` values reviewed first** (point 5
  above) and the verification-coverage gaps (point 7) treated as a
  tracked follow-up, not a blocker to import itself -- these are
  evidence-completeness gaps on already-`VERIFIED`/`HUMAN_REVIEW` facts,
  not schema or referential defects.
- **Connecting the GHL frontend: NO-GO at this stage.** No frontend
  integration work, query design against the imported dataset, or GHL-
  facing contract was scoped or built in this synthesis; it was
  explicitly out of this skill's system-context boundary (Sanity /
  Supabase / GHL are separated responsibilities, and this synthesis
  touched only the Sanity-bound schema and data).
- **Beginning a first 10-20 university batch: NO-GO until (a) this
  freeze report and the Sanity import above are reviewed and signed off,
  and (b) the verification-coverage backlog (point 7) has a stated plan
  -- either resolved for these five institutions first, or explicitly
  accepted as an ongoing parallel workstream that a batch's own
  `check_verification_coverage_v1_2.py` results will be measured against
  the same way.** The batch contract itself (`06-batch-ingestion/`) is
  ready the moment that sign-off happens; nothing further needs to be
  designed before a real batch begins.

**No live Sanity write occurred. No sixth university was researched. No
batch ingestion began.** This freeze report, the Sanity package, the
batch design, and the directory strategy are all deliverables of this
single synthesis pass, produced entirely from the five institutions'
already-gathered evidence.
