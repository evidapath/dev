# Oxford: V1.1 → V1.2 migration report

## ID stability

No canonical ID changed. All V1.1 IDs (`gb-university-of-oxford`, `gb-university-of-oxford-central`, `gb-university-of-oxford-ba-computer-science`, all `ApplicantCategory`/`AdmissionsRequirement`/`Scholarship`/`SourceRecord`/`DerivedCostEstimate` IDs) are preserved unchanged. Two V1.1 `CostProfile` IDs survive (`-cost-home`, `-cost-overseas`); one (`-cost-visa-maintenance-funds`) is retired as a `CostProfile` and its content relocated to a new `FinancialRequirement` record — see below. No `v1.1-to-v1.2-id-remap.json` is produced (no ID was renamed, only one record's *entity type* changed).

## Structural fixes applied

1. **`Program.campus_ref` fabrication removed** (gm-11): V1.1's `gb-university-of-oxford-central` campus was explicitly documented as a synthetic "Oxford as a place" record created only to satisfy V1.1's required `campus_ref` field, not a real bounded campus. `Program.campus_ref` is now `null`. The `Campus` record itself is retained unchanged (it may still be useful as a City-level reference point), but the Program no longer claims a false single-campus relationship.
2. **UCAS deadline / code promoted out of `extension_metadata`**: `Program.extension_metadata.fields.ucas_code` and `.application_deadline` are now a structured `DecisionPlan` record (`gb-university-of-oxford-decisionplan-2027`, `application_platform: "UCAS"`) plus `Program.application_platform` and `Program.application_requirements[]` (UCAS application, TMUA, interview). No data lost — the UCAS code itself (G400) is retained in `Program.application_requirements[0].notes`.
3. **Interview process promoted to `AdmissionStage`**: the interview-and-cross-college-reallocation-pool process, previously repeated as identical free text across three `AdmissionsRequirement.notes` fields, is now one `AdmissionStage` record (`gb-university-of-oxford-stage-interview`), referenced via `admission_stage_ref` from all three A-Level/IB/Advanced-Highers `AdmissionsRequirement` records (the fourth, AP-US, has no confirmed stage since its own record remains `UNKNOWN`/`HUMAN_REVIEW`).
4. **BA→MCompSci integrated continuation promoted to `ProgramPathway`** (gm-06/gm-20): previously only describable via `Program.degree_type_local` free text. Now a `ProgramPathway` record (`gb-university-of-oxford-pathway-mcompsci`, `pathway_type: integrated_continuation`) with a structured `progression_requirement` (2:1 or higher in year-3 examinations, decision point end of year 3).
5. **Monthly living-cost range promoted** (gm-10): `CostProfile.extension_metadata.fields.living_cost_monthly_range` (GBP 1,475–2,175/month) is now the canonical `CostProfile.estimated_living_costs_monthly_range` field on both the Home and Overseas cost profiles. The remaining extension facts (college fee bundling note, tuition range context) stay in `extension_metadata` since they have no other canonical home yet.
6. **Visa maintenance-funds threshold relocated to `FinancialRequirement`** (gm-15/gm-16): the V1.1 `gb-university-of-oxford-cost-visa-maintenance-funds` `CostProfile` record (a UK Home Office Student-visa financial threshold, explicitly NOT a tuition figure and NOT Oxford's own living-cost guidance) is retired as a `CostProfile` and re-created as a `FinancialRequirement` (`gb-university-of-oxford-finreq-visa-maintenance`, `requirement_type: government_minimum_resources`). Because the published figure is a range (GBP 1,171–1,529/month depending on region) rather than a single number, `amount` is left `null` and the full range is preserved in `extension_metadata.fields.visa_maintenance_monthly_range` — no data lost, only relocated out of the `CostProfile` collection where it structurally never belonged (it isn't a cost-of-living estimate; it's an immigration liquidity threshold, the same category as TU Delft's IND deposit and TU Munich's Sperrkonto).
7. **`Scholarship.college_ref`**: added as `null` on both Oxford scholarships (Reach Oxford, Oxford Bursary) — neither is college-administered per the source evidence (Reach Oxford is university-wide across colleges; the Oxford Bursary is "standardized centrally... regardless of college").

## College entity: justified but not populated this migration

Oxford is the primary structural evidence for the new `College` entity (gm-04) — the fee-bundling formula ("fees... divided between the University and your college on a formula basis") and the college-specific hardship-fund variance discovered via `src-09` both point at a real, material college-level distinction. However, **zero `College` records are populated** in this migration: the only individual college names that surfaced in the V1.1 run (St Anne's, St Catherine's) came from `src-09`, a `secondary`-type source used explicitly for corroboration only, never as the cited authority for a structured field, per the project's source hierarchy. Fabricating named `College` records from a secondary source, or inventing generic placeholder colleges, would violate the no-fabrication rule. This is flagged as a genuine follow-up research item for a future Oxford re-run — not evidence against the College entity's design, which remains justified by the *structural* facts already independently verified (the fee-bundling statement itself is from `src-02`, an official University of Oxford page).

## Two `DerivedCostEstimate` records retired (not lost)

V1.1's `derived-visa-funds-total-low-9mo` and `derived-visa-funds-total-high-9mo` were EvidaPath-calculated annualizations of the UK visa maintenance-funds monthly range, referencing the retired `gb-university-of-oxford-cost-visa-maintenance-funds` `CostProfile` via `cost_profile_ref`. Since that record is no longer a `CostProfile` in V1.2 (it is now `FinancialRequirement` `gb-university-of-oxford-finreq-visa-maintenance`), and `DerivedCostEstimate.cost_profile_ref` is specifically typed to reference a `CostProfile`, these two derived calculations are retired rather than retargeted to a mismatched entity type. **No source fact is lost**: the underlying GBP 1,171/1,529-per-month government figures, and the calculation method that produced these two derived totals, are fully preserved in the `FinancialRequirement` record's `extension_metadata.fields.visa_maintenance_monthly_range`. The four remaining `DerivedCostEstimate` records (Oxford's own living-cost annualizations, tied to the Home/Overseas `CostProfile` records) are unaffected and carried forward unchanged.

## Unmapped facts

None. Every V1.1 field, including every `extension_metadata` entry, has a home in V1.2: either unchanged, relocated to a canonical field, promoted to a new structured entity, or (for the two derived visa-funds calculations above) retired with its underlying source data fully preserved elsewhere.

## VerificationRecord formalization

`verification-records-v1.2.json` in this folder carries forward the original V1.1 run's field-level `VerificationRecord`s, reformatted to the V1.2 schema: any record with the informal `absence_claim: true` flag had its `verification_status` promoted to the now-formal `VERIFIED_ABSENT` enum value; `material_extension_fact` is preserved as the now-formal schema field (unchanged in meaning); any NYUAD-run-specific informal semantic-QA flags (`financial_aid_semantic_reviewed`, `holistic_admissions_semantic_reviewed` -- not part of the V1.2 schema) were folded into `conflict_or_limitation_notes` as a bracketed annotation. This is a reformatting pass only -- no field-level claim was added, removed, or re-verified.
