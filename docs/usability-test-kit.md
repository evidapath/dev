# EvidaPath — Usability Test Kit (P2)

**Acceptance test:** five target-age students complete the core journey **without coaching**.
**Who:** students aged 16–18 in their final two years of school (IB, A-Level, American or other), ideally in the UAE.
Mix curricula and nationalities (at least one Emirati, one expat). Nobody who has seen EvidaPath before.
**Time:** 30 minutes per student. One facilitator, one note-taker if possible.

---

## Before the session

- **Consent:** participants are minors, so get written consent from a parent or guardian (and the student) to take
  part and to record the screen. Don't record faces unless consent covers it.
- **Accounts:** each student creates their own account during the test (that is part of the journey). Use a fresh
  browser profile so no one else is signed in. Afterwards, delete the test accounts or keep them only with consent.
- **Device:** whatever they normally use. Test at least two of the five on a phone.
- **URL:** the staging site once it exists; until then a laptop running the local app.

## What to say (read it word for word)

> "We're testing the website, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think out loud: say what you're looking
> for, what you expect to happen and what confuses you. I can't help you during the tasks, because we need to see where
> the site fails to help you. If you get stuck you can say 'I'd give up here', and that's useful too."

**Facilitator rules:** don't point, don't name buttons, don't say "nearly" or "try the menu". If asked, answer
"What would you do if I weren't here?" Only step in if they're stuck for **2 minutes**; then mark the task *failed*
and move them on.

---

## Tasks (read one at a time; these deliberately avoid the site's button names)

| # | Task as read to the student | Success looks like | Journey stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Using this site, find some universities that could realistically suit you." | Completes the Find My Path questions and reaches a results list | Discover |
| 2 | "Pick one result that interests you and find out what it would cost per year as an international student." | States a yearly figure **and** notices whether it's verified or pending | Analyze |
| 3 | "Find out what grades or qualifications that university wants from someone with your curriculum." | States the requirement, or correctly says none is published | Analyze |
| 4 | "Keep two universities you'd like to come back to later." | Two universities saved while signed in (creates an account if needed) | Plan |
| 5 | "Leave yourself a reminder about one of them." | A note saved on a saved university | Plan |
| 6 | "Find out whether you could get funding to study at one of your saved universities." | Finds a relevant scholarship and says whether it's **guaranteed or competitive** and whether they're eligible | Fund |
| 7 | "You've just applied to one of them. Record that." | Application added with status *submitted* | Plan |
| 8 | "Sign out. Now come back and check your universities are still there." | Signs in and finds both saved universities and the note | Persistence |
| 9 | "If you wanted to talk to a real person about your choices, how would you do it?" | Finds and submits the counseling waitlist (use a test entry, or their real details with consent) | Counseling |

**Core journey = tasks 1, 2, 4, 6, 8.** The acceptance test passes when **all five students complete all five core
tasks without help**. Tasks 3, 5, 7 and 9 are tracked but don't decide pass or fail.

**Trust checks (ask after task 6):**
1. "How confident are you that the cost you found is correct? Why?" (1–5)
2. "What does 'Pending verified data' mean to you?"
3. "Would you or your parents make a decision based on this site? What would you check elsewhere first?"

**Wrap-up (2 minutes):** "What was the most confusing moment?" · "What did you expect to find that wasn't there?" ·
"Would you use this again? Why or why not?"

---

## After all five sessions

- List every problem that **two or more** students hit — those come first.
- For each, note the task, what happened, a quote, and the page.
- **Pay attention to any misreading of a fact** (a cost, eligibility or deadline). EvidaPath's whole value is
  trustworthy facts, so a student misreading a verified figure is as serious as a wrong figure.
- Fix, then re-test the failed tasks with two new students.
