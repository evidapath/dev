# EvidaPath — 15 student walkthroughs (2026-10-31)

**How this was done:** each student went through the live local build (latest code, Sanity data including the
2026-10-31 fee and curriculum corrections) as a real user would: answered the Find My Path questions, read the
results, opened the analysis for their top pick. Five runs were at phone width (390px). Ratings are "would this
help me decide, and would I trust it?" out of 10.

## 1. Aisha — Emirati, UAE Ministry of Education 90–94%, Computer Science, $20k, wants the UAE — **4/10**
**What I saw:** "Nothing in United Arab Emirates fits $20k/yr." Top 3: University of Malaya (tuition only, $6.3k),
University of Sharjah ($10.1k/yr short), Taylor's (tuition only). "Fits your budget" starts with LMU Munich — taught
in German. NYU Abu Dhabi: "$1.8k/yr short — no scholarship found yet".
**What worked:** the 4-question intake took seconds; the NYUAD card did apply her guaranteed Sheikh Mohamed bin
Zayed scholarship (net $21.8k/yr).
**What went wrong:**
- As an Emirati she'd expect the federal universities to be cheap or free. **UAEU and Khalifa are priced at the
  international rate**; Khalifa offers UAE nationals full or partial tuition scholarships plus housing, and UAEU is
  publicly funded for nationals. Zayed University and HCT aren't in EvidaPath at all. So she's sent to Malaysia.
- "No scholarship found yet" on NYUAD is wrong-sounding: her guaranteed scholarship *is* counted. It should say
  "$1.8k/yr short even after your guaranteed scholarship".
- Two of her top 3 are tuition-only Malaysian universities in a country she didn't pick.
**As Aisha:** "It told me nothing in the UAE fits and to go to Malaysia. That's not true for Emiratis."

## 2. Rohan — Indian expat in Dubai, CBSE 85–89%, Computer Science, $30k, anywhere (phone) — **7/10**
**What I saw:** "7 fit your budget. 18 more cost a bit more." Top 2: Monash Malaysia ($21.1k, fits, meets grades),
University of Malaya ($6.3k tuition). Analysis: "Fits your budget", meets the CBSE 75% requirement, $8.9k left over,
"Do this next: check Monash High Achiever Award".
**What worked:** CBSE is now understood — Monash Malaysia correctly says he meets 75%. The verdict + cost bar made
the decision obvious in seconds.
**What went wrong:**
- On a phone the filters fill the whole first screen; the top picks start below the fold.
- University of Sharjah shows as "$144/yr short" in "A bit over budget" — for $144 that's effectively a fit.
- Only a top 2: the "best match for your grades" pick was the same university as "best you can afford".
**As Rohan:** "Clear and quick. I'd shortlist Monash Malaysia straight away."

## 3. Sara — Pakistani, A-levels A*AA, Economics, $40k, wants the UK — **6/10**

Nottingham ($10k/yr short, meets grades), University of Malaya (lowest cost). 7 UK options "a bit over budget".
**What worked:** honest that UK costs (tuition + official living cost) exceed $40k; Nottingham correctly "meets grades".
**What went wrong:**
- Her #1 pick is in Germany and LMU Economics needs **German B2** — the card doesn't say so, because the programme is
  tagged as English + German. A UK-focused student shouldn't get a German university as her top pick.
- "Lowest cost" is again Malaya — tuition only, in a country she never mentioned.
**As Sara:** "I asked for the UK and my top pick is in Munich and needs German?"

## What to fix, in order

1. **Top-3 picks (affects 11 of 15).**
   - Stay in the chosen country and language when possible. Never pick a German-taught programme for someone who
     didn't say they speak German, and never make an out-of-country, tuition-only option "best you can afford" if a
     fully priced option exists at home.
   - "Best match for your grades" only when the grades actually meet the published requirement; otherwise call it
     "Closest option" or drop it.
   - With **no budget limit**, rank by academic fit and drop "best you can afford" / "lowest cost".
2. **Citizenship-aware prices (Aisha, Yusuf, Mei, James).** We store residency tiers but always show the
   international one. Emiratis at UAEU/Khalifa, Malaysians at Malaysian universities and EU students in the
   Netherlands should see their own fee — and Khalifa's Emirati scholarship should count.
3. **"Which languages can you study in?" (Lena, Sara, Noor).** Default English; pre-tick German for Abitur.
4. **Abitur grade scale (1.0–4.0)** and Abitur requirements for German universities.
5. **"Just within reach" for small gaps** (under ~5% of the budget: Sharjah $144, Twente $604).
6. **Wording:** "short even after your guaranteed scholarship" when guaranteed aid is already applied.
7. **Subjects we don't cover (Priya):** say which subjects we do cover, offer "notify me" / counselor waitlist.
8. **Phone layout:** collapse the filters into a one-line summary so the top picks are above the fold.
9. **More funding sources:** UAE government scholarships for Emiratis abroad; flag need-based aid at US
   universities (MIT/Harvard/Princeton meet full need for internationals).
10. **Data gaps found:** Zayed University and HCT missing; UAE MoE minimum averages for UAEU/AUS/CUD/RIT; US-diploma
    requirements for Canadian universities; compulsory fees for McMaster, Nottingham Malaysia, Universiti Malaya,
    Taylor's.

Items 1, 5, 6, 7 and 8 are code-only and quick. Items 2–4 need small data-model work. Items 9–10 are research.
