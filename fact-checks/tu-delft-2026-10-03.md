## The real finding: data was CORRECT; the ADAPTER was wrong
- Sanity holds **two** cost profiles (EU statutory €2,694 / non-EU institutional €19,906) — accurate and careful, **zero hallucination**.
- **Bug (now fixed):** `getUniversitiesFromSanity` picked `costProfile[0]` (the EU rate), so the app showed **€2,694 to everyone incl. UAE students who pay €19,906** — a ~7x understatement to the core audience. NYUAD hid this (single uniform profile).
- **Fix:** `selectCost()` now shows the **highest (international) tier** — never understate a family's cost — and adds `costResidencyNote` disclosing the lower tier ("…Lower tiers pay from EUR 2,694/yr"), surfaced on the Universities card. Verified via live query: adapter now shows €19,906 for TU Delft.

## Verdict
TU Delft's displayed facts are verified against primary sources, for BOTH residency
tiers, and the app now shows the correct rate for international students with the EU
rate disclosed. `nl-tu-delft` stays in FACT_CHECKED. The residency-tier fix also
corrects any other school priced this way (checked per-school as we verify them).
