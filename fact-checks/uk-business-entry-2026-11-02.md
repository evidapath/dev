## UCL: which course

The UCL record's description said "BSc Management or BSc International Management", with one 2026-27 fee. UCL has no
plain "Management BSc"; the School of Management's general business degree is International Management BSc (N290)
(Management Science BSc, N991, is the quantitative one and needs A* in Mathematics). The programme is renamed
"International Management" and pointed at the N290 page.
