## Programs (4 pillars) + program-specific subject minimums (grade equiv. B)
- **Computer Science** — BSCS, College of Engineering (CSE dept), ABET(CAC). Admission: **Math + a Science**.
- **Computer Engineering** — BSCoE, College of Engineering, ABET(EAC). Admission: **Math + Physics** (Physics by Grade 11 min).
- **Business Administration** — BSBA, School of Business Administration (AACSB). Admission: **Math**.
- **Economics** — BAE (Bachelor of Arts in Economics), SBA / Dept of Economics (BSBA-Econ also exists). Admission: **Math** (SBA).

## Scholarships (guaranteed vs competitive — distinguished)
- **President's Scholarship** — competitive merit; 100% tuition + lab fees + stipend/housing/travel/insurance; IELTS 7.0/TOEFL 94; e.g. American 98% CGPA G10-11 + 3 AP grade-5 or SAT 1400; deadline Apr 1.
- **Chancellor's Scholarship** — competitive merit; full tuition + lab fees + housing/insurance; slightly below President's (2 AP grade-5 or SAT 1200).
- **Merit Scholarships** — automatic at admission (not competitive); 10–20% tuition + lab fees; 95% avg G10-11; transfers ineligible; not guaranteed.
- **Family Tuition Grant** — need-based, guaranteed if eligible; 25% discount for 2nd+ sibling enrolled simultaneously.
