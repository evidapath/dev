Why: the user found hallucinations and loopholes in the data. Every student-facing figure is being re-read on the
university's (or government's) own page, today, and each staged change carries the quote and URL it came from
(a `sourceRecord-ev-*` document written in the same file).

Pending files: `fact-checks/pending/2026-11-02-reverify-<university>.ndjson` and
`fact-checks/pending/2026-11-02-uae-living-costs.ndjson`. Backups of every document touched are in the session
scratchpad (`backup-reverify-<university>.json`, `backup-uae-living-2026-11-02.json`).

## Rules used

Living costs, in order: (1) the university's own living-cost estimate, saying what it assumes; (2) a government
figure for students (UK, Australia, Germany, Canada visas); (3) the university's own housing price, labelled
"housing only"; (4) nothing. Never blogs or agencies ("regional advisory aggregates"), never another university's
figure.

Status: rows checked against the live page are set `verification_status: VERIFIED` with `last_checked: 2026-11-02`;
where the page says the thing isn't published, `VERIFIED_ABSENT`. Anything not re-checked will be marked
`UNKNOWN` / `HUMAN_REVIEW` at the end so the site can say "not checked yet".

Derived numbers say so in `academic_year` (e.g. per-credit rate x credits, or programme total / 4).
