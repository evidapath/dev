# University of Alberta — Canadian fees added, international fees confirmed — 2026-11-02

## Why

In the 50-student run, Noah (Canadian, Canada) saw Alberta as "Cost not published": Alberta only had international
profiles. Three of those were marked "from secondary sources … verify from ualberta.ca", and Economics had no fee.

## Source

UAlberta Office of the Registrar's Undergraduate Cost Calculator (https://costcalculator.registrar.ualberta.ca/),
run in headed Chrome for each programme as a Canadian and as an international student, living off campus. It is
the page UAlberta's own "Sample Fall / Winter Tuition Assessment" link opens.

| Programme (our record) | Calculator programme | Canadian tuition, Fall 2026/Winter 2027 | International tuition, Fall 2026 (guaranteed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Computing Science | BSc with Specialization (30 units) | $7,293.60 | $39,792.00 (was $39,792, now confirmed) |
| Economics | Bachelor of Arts (30 units) | $7,293.60 | $39,232.80 (was missing) |
| Engineering | BSc in Mechanical Engineering (41 units) | $9,655.68 | $49,952.40 (was $49,952, now confirmed) |
| Commerce | Bachelor of Commerce (30 units) | $10,674.72 | $46,546.80 (was $46,547, now confirmed) |

Canadian tuition is course-based, so the calculator prices a standard first-year load. For Science and Arts it matches
the Registrar's published rate of $729.36 per 3-credit course (10 courses = $7,293.60). Commerce and Engineering mix
standard and faculty-rate courses ($1,152 Business, $1,123.04 Engineering), so those totals are taken from the
calculator as shown. International
students have no general first-year Engineering option in the calculator, so Mechanical Engineering is used for both.

"Tuition is accurate for the Fall 2026 academic year." Fall 2027 rates were not yet published, so the records stay
2026-2027.

## Records

- New category "Domestic (Canadian citizens and permanent residents)", which the app treats as the domestic tier for
  Canadian citizens studying in Canada.
- Four new domestic cost profiles. Non-instructional fees and living cost are copied from the international profiles
  (the living figure is the IRCC estimate; it's labelled as such and applies to a student living away from home).
- Four international profiles: tuition set from the calculator, notes saying "secondary sources / verify" replaced.

Not changed: UBC, McMaster, Waterloo and McGill still have international fees only, so Canadians see "Cost not
published" there too. Same fix applies.
